Source-based iOS/iPadOS Metal effects library with an Arial UIKit overlay,
effect selection, parameter controls, ReShade INI import/save, and bundled
shaders, textures, and Extravi presets. The first launch works offline.

This is an experimental device build. Re-sign the dylib using LiveContainer's
Tweaks → Sign before loading it. Effect compatibility and depth capture depend
on the host renderer and device; compilation errors are shown in the overlay.
This release does not include a Roblox IPA or Maxey's framework.

Third-party shaders and presets retain their authors and source notices.
qUINT: Pascal Gilcher / Marty McFly. Extravi presets: Extravi. ReShadeFX:
crosire. SPIRV-Cross: Khronos and contributors.
