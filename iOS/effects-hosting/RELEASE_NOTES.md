## RoMacShade iOS Effect Library v1

Initial library for the source-based RoMacShade iOS/iPadOS dylib. The compressed
pack contains the ReShade effect files, includes, textures, and presets used by
the app. It includes the 17 supplied Extravi presets and two RoMacShade samples.

The matching app can use the library offline from its embedded copy. It can also
download this pack from this release to restore or refresh its installed copy.
The app validates the whole-pack and per-file SHA-256 digests before replacing
the installed library.

Third-party work retains its source headers and author attribution. This release
does not include a Roblox IPA or a prebuilt dylib.
